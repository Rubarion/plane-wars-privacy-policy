Privacy Policy for Plane Wars: VR Airplane Dogfight
Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview
This Privacy Policy applies to the virtual reality application Plane Wars: VR Airplane Dogfight ("the Application"), developed by Rubarion Games ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Plane Wars: VR Airplane Dogfight is designed to function completely offline and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information.

2. Information Collection and Use
No Personal Data Collection: The Application does not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, device ID, or location).

No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not track user behavior, gameplay metrics, session lengths, or crash reports through any third-party analytics services.

No Internet Access Required: The Application operates entirely on your Meta Quest headset without requiring an active internet connection or external server communication during gameplay.

3. Local Saved Data
Any game settings, progress, or high scores achieved within the Application are stored locally on your Meta Quest device. This data remains on your physical device and is never uploaded to external servers or accessed by us. You can clear or delete this data at any time by uninstalling the Application or clearing the app data through your headset settings.

4. Third-Party Services & Headset Permissions
The Application relies solely on the native Meta Quest operating environment (Meta Horizon OS) to function. We do not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or external analytics frameworks.

The Application uses standard Meta Quest Touch controller tracking and headset position tracking solely to render local in-game flight controls and VR movement. None of this tracking data is recorded or transmitted.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)
Because the Application collects zero personal data from any user, it does not collect information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local legal age). The game is fully safe and privacy-compliant for users of all permitted age groups on the Meta Quest platform.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if new features are added in future updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
